About Us

We're the Tails From Home family: Mimi, Grace, and Griffin. We started by helping friends and neighbors, and we treat every dog we watch like our own. Whether it's a quick walk or a week-long stay, your pup gets our full attention and love, and you'll always get an update to know they're happy, settled, and in good hands.

Services

Dog Walking

Daily walks to keep your dog happy, active and tired out in the best way.

Drop-In Visits

Quick check-ins for feeding, potty breaks, meds and playtime.

Boarding

Your dog stays with our family and is treated like part of it.

Overnight Sitting

We stay at your home so your dog keeps their routine and stays calm.
