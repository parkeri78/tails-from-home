About Us

We're the Tails From Home family: Mimi, Grace, Griffin, and Parker. We started by helping friends and neighbors, and we treat every pet we care for like our own. Whether it's a quick walk or a week-long stay, your pet gets our full attention and love, and you'll always get an update to know they're happy, settled, and in good hands.

Services

Dog Walking

Drop-In Visits

Boarding

Overnight Sitting
